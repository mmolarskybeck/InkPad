# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Interactive fiction writers, game developers, and storytellers working with Inkle Studios' Ink language. They range from hobbyists exploring narrative design to professional game writers managing complex branching narratives. They work on personal devices, in browsers, and value frictionless access—no installation, no auth barriers, no bloat.

## Product Purpose

InkPad is a complete, browser-based editor for Ink story development. It eliminates friction: write in the browser, compile instantly, test interactively, inspect variables, debug in real-time—all client-side, no backend required. The goal is to make Ink development feel native, fast, and elegant so writers focus on story, not tooling.

## Brand Personality

**Sleek, elegant, snappy.**

- **Sleek**: Minimal surface, dark and refined, every interaction feels considered and responsive. Not playful; not corporate. Crafted restraint.
- **Elegant**: Sophisticated, purposeful, respect the craft. Inspired by writing-first tools (Obsidian, Scrivener) where design gets out of the way.
- **Snappy**: Instant feedback, no lag, real-time compilation, immediate visual response. The tool feels alive.

## Anti-references

**Avoid:**
- Cluttered UIs with too many affordances competing for attention.
- Overly "designed" or flashy interfaces (trendy gradients, motion for show, excessive color).
- Clunky, heavy, or sluggish interactions.
- Corporate or corporate-adjacent aesthetics (rigid grids, sanitized charts, "modern sans" everywhere).

**Embrace instead:**
- The restraint and focus of Obsidian's sidebar-driven layout.
- The clarity and hierarchy of VS Code's dual-panel editor.
- Scrivener's respect for the writer's workflow (organization without distraction).

## Design Principles

1. **Respect the writer's focus.** Every UI element must earn its place; distraction is the enemy.
2. **Show, don't tell.** Real-time feedback, visual feedback for every action, no modal dialogs where a panel would do.
3. **Craft over trend.** Timeless, refined design that won't feel dated in 2 years. Embrace dark theme and understated elegance.
4. **Speed is a feature.** Instant compilation, instant visual response, zero perceived latency. Performance is aesthetic.
5. **Semantic clarity.** Errors, warnings, syntax, states—each must be instantly legible without requiring a legend.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG 2.1 AA** as the minimum standard.
- All interactive elements must be keyboard-navigable; focus must be visible and managed intentionally.
- Color alone must never convey information; support reduced motion in all animations and transitions.
- Text contrast must be ≥4.5:1 for body copy, ≥3:1 for large text.
- Error messages and warnings must be explicit and actionable, never relying on color cues alone.
