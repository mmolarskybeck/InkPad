InkPad

InkPad is a free, local-first online editor for writing, testing, and exporting interactive stories made with inkle's ink narrative scripting language. It runs entirely in your browser and needs no account or install. Your projects stay on your device.

Write ink with syntax highlighting, snippets, and live error checking. Play your story in a built-in preview, inspect variables as they change, and export a playable HTML file or compiled JSON.

Learn more on the InkPad page at Shadowbox Games or browse the source on GitHub.
